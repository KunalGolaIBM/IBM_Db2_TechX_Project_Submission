- **False-allow rate**: drafts that are wrong for their date but were released as PASS, over all wrong drafts. The safety number.
- **False-block rate**: correct drafts that were rewritten or escalated, over all correct drafts.

- **Citation accuracy**: the key claim was checked against the right clause version for the date.
- **Rule overrides**: claims the deterministic rule check turned from the judge's SUPPORTED to CONTRADICTED.
