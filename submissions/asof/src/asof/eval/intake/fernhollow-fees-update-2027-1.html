Fernhollow Bank

Personal Account Fee Schedule: Update 2027-1

Summary of fee changes. Prepared by Retail Banking Pricing.

These changes take effect on 1 February 2027. Fees not listed here are unchanged.

1. Wire transfers

1.1 Outgoing domestic wire transfers

An outgoing domestic wire transfer costs $25.

2. ATM fees

2.1 Out-of-network ATM fee (temporary reduction)

From 1 March 2027 until 31 August 2027, we charge $3 for each withdrawal at an ATM outside the Fernhollow network. The ATM's owner may charge its own fee as well.

3. Checks

3.1 Cashier's checks (new fee)

A cashier's check costs $10.

4. Statements

4.1 Paper statements (restated, no change)

Paper statements cost $2 per statement. Electronic statements are free.
