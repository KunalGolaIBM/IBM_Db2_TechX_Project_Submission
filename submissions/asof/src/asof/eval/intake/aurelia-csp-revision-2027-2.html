Customer Service Plan: Revision 2027-2

Summary of changes. Owner: Customer Experience Policy.

This revision takes effect on 15 January 2027. Sections not listed here are unchanged.

1. Bereavement fares

1.1 Eligibility and discount

Bereavement fares are available to customers travelling because of the death or imminent death of an immediate family member. Immediate family means a spouse or common-law partner, parent, step-parent, child, step-child, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or legal guardian. The bereavement fare is 50% off the lowest available Economy fare on the same flight.

2. Travel credits

2.1 Who can use a travel credit

Travel credits are issued in the name of the original passenger and may be transferred once to another person.

3. Delay compensation

3.1 Delays of 9 hours or more

For a delay within Aurelia Air's control that makes the passenger arrive 9 hours or more late, the passenger is entitled to $450 in compensation.
