Aurelia Air

Customer Service Plan: Revision 2027-1

Summary of changes. Owner: Customer Experience Policy. Approved for publication on 12 January 2027.

This revision of the Aurelia Air Customer Service Plan takes effect on 1 February 2027. Unless a section says otherwise, each change applies to tickets issued on or after that date. Sections not listed here are unchanged.

1. Baggage

1.1 First checked bag fee

The first checked bag costs $45 each way on Economy fares.

1.2 Sports equipment (new section)

One set of golf clubs, skis or a snowboard may be checked in place of a standard bag for a fee of $75 each way. The equipment may weigh up to 23 kg.

2. Travel credits

2.1 Travel credit validity

This section applies to travel credits issued on or after 1 March 2027.

Travel credits are valid for 24 months from the date they are issued. The booking must be made before the credit expires, but the flight itself may take place after the expiry date.

3. Delay compensation

3.1 Claiming delay compensation (restated, no change)

Delay compensation must be claimed within 1 year of the flight date through the online claims form.
