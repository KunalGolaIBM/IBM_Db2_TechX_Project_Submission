Citizen Escalation Protocol

If your public grievance is not resolved within charter limits, follow these escalation stages.

  1. Step 1: Standard Department Review (0–12 Days)

    The concerned ministry or department reviews your case file and assigns a designated redressal officer.

  2. Step 2: 12-Day Online Portal Escalation

    If your grievance remains in "Under Review" for 12 days, use the Escalate This Grievance button on the Track Status page to flag your case directly to the Appellate Nodal Authority.

  3. Step 3: Appellate Authority Review

    The Senior Joint Secretary / Appellate Authority reviews the unaddressed delay and issues binding instructions to the zonal officer.

  4. Step 4: Formal Representation to DARPG

    If extraordinary circumstances persist, you may submit formal representation citing your registration number to the Secretary (DARPG).

Notice regarding fraudulent agents: CPGRAMS is completely free of charge. No intermediary or agent is authorized by the Government of India to demand payment for grievance redressal.
