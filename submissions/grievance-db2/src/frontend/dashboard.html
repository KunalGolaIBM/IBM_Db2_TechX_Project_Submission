IBM Db2 Technical Depth: Date Math, Indexing & Stored Procedures

Unlike standard databases requiring complex epoch conversions, IBM Db2 natively evaluates continuous civic service charters via calendar day arithmetic DAYS(CURRENT DATE) - DAYS(DATE(created_at)). Combined with compound B-Tree indexes on (status, created_at, ward) and the native stored procedure SP_ESCALATE_OVERDUE, SLA breaches are monitored and escalated on-the-fly directly inside the Db2 engine.
