Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about filing, tracking, and escalating public grievances.

1. Filing a Grievance

Who is eligible to file a grievance on CPGRAMS?

Any citizen of India can file a grievance related to central government departments, ministries, public sector banks, and PSUs. You do not need any special registration or fee. You can file on behalf of yourself or a family member with their consent.

How does the Voice Input and Suggested Department feature work?

On the Lodge Grievance page, click the 🎙️ Voice Input (Speak) button to dictate your issue. As you describe your problem, our keyword engine automatically detects relevant departments (e.g. typing "ration card" suggests the Department of Food & Public Distribution) and allows 1-click department selection.

What information should I provide for fastest resolution?

Please include: a clear subject, specific dates, previous application/reference/acknowledgment numbers, the exact location/office involved, and what resolution you expect. You can also attach up to 3 supporting PDF or image files.

Is there any fee or charge for filing a grievance?

No. CPGRAMS is an official service of the Government of India and is 100% free. Never pay anyone claiming to expedite or file your grievance.

2. Tracking & 12-Day Escalation Workflow

How do I track my submitted grievance?

Visit the Track Status page and enter your registration number (format: GRV-2026-XXXX or CPGRM/2025/XXXXXXX). You will see a visual 4-stage milestone timeline: Filed → Under Review → Forwarded to Department → Resolved.

What is the 12-Day Escalation Feature?

Under Citizen Charter guidelines, if your grievance has been pending in Under Review for more than 12 days without an official response, an Escalate This Grievance option appears on your status card. Clicking it flags your case as High Priority and forwards it to the Senior Appellate Nodal Officer.

Where do I find my registration tracking number?

Your registration number is displayed immediately upon submitting on the confirmation screen and is sent via SMS and Email to your registered contact details.

3. Excluded Matters

Can I file grievances about state government or municipal issues?

CPGRAMS handles grievances related to central government ministries and organizations. For municipal or state-specific matters (like local property tax or state police), please file on your state's respective CM helpline portal.

Can I file RTI (Right to Information) requests here?

No. RTI requests should be filed on the RTI Online portal at rtionline.gov.in. CPGRAMS is designated strictly for grievance redressal.

Can I file matters that are currently pending in court (Sub-judice)?

No. Matters pending in any court of law or judicial tribunal cannot be adjudicated through CPGRAMS.

4. Appeals & Feedback

What if I am not satisfied with the department's resolution?

If you are not satisfied with the resolution order, you can file an Appeal within 30 days of closure through the Track Status page for review by an Appellate Authority.

Can I provide feedback on officer handling?

Yes. Once your grievance reaches the Resolved stage, a feedback rating prompt enables citizens to rate the timeliness and quality of resolution.

Still need assistance?

Our citizen grievance support desk is available to assist you during working hours.
