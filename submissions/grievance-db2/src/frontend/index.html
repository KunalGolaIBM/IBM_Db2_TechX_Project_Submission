Official Government Portal

File a complaint about a government service and track its progress online.

CPGRAMS makes it easy for every citizen to raise public grievances related to central government departments and ministries, and track them until resolution.

How CPGRAMS Works

Three simple steps to file and resolve your grievance

1

Submit Your Grievance

Fill out a simple form describing your issue with a central government service. You can also use voice input and attach supporting documents.

2

Track Progress

Use your registration number to check the status of your grievance at any time. You'll see which department is handling your case.

3

Receive a Response

The concerned department reviews and addresses your grievance. You'll be notified of the outcome and can escalate if delayed.

Can I file this grievance?

CPGRAMS handles grievances related to central government services. Here's a quick guide.

You CAN file about

  • Delays in government services (passport, pension, ration, etc.)
  • Issues with central government employees' conduct
  • Problems with central government schemes or DBT benefits
  • Complaints about public sector banks, insurance, and post
  • Issues with railways, telecom, highways, power boards
  • Misuse of authority or corruption

Not handled by CPGRAMS

  • Matters pending in courts of law (Sub-judice)
  • RTI (Right to Information) requests (file at rtionline.gov.in)
  • Purely state government or local municipal disputes
  • Private consumer or purely commercial contracts
  • Matters related to armed forces strategic operations
